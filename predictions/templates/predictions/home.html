Common loan factors

These are the details that usually carry the most weight in a loan decision.

  • Income level Higher applicant and coapplicant income can strengthen repayment capacity.
  • Credit history A positive credit history is usually one of the strongest indicators in the model.
  • Loan amount The requested amount is reviewed against income and expected repayment capacity.
  • Loan term Term length helps show whether the repayment schedule is realistic for the borrower.
